This directory holds a practical, advanced page for readers who already know what RDF and SPARQL are (from [`intro/`](../intro/) and [`basic/`](../basic/)) and now want to publish their own dataset as RDF. It's not about UniProt or Rhea specifically - it's the checklist of decisions and pitfalls that applies to publishing any life science resource as linked data: choosing identifiers, reusing existing vocabulary, content negotiation, SHACL shapes, and making data queryable.

It points back at this repository's own `basic/` example data (`ontology.ttl`, `resource.ttl`, `shapes.ttl`) as a real, small, worked case of these choices.

Read it on the published site, or as plain Markdown in [tutorial.md](./tutorial.md).
